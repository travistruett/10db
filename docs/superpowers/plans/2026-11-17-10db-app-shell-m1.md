# App shell M1: the rail, the panel, the title row and one surface

## Brief

**What changes for you.**
- Every screen sits in the new frame:
  - an icon rail, with names in tooltips to the right and the status icons and Settings at its foot;
  - a panel column (only Settings has one in M1);
  - a title row level with the traffic lights, holding each screen's title and actions;
  - the content on one rounded surface, in tone C.
- #645's heavy shadows become a hairline.
- Under 1000 px the panel folds behind a sidebar button (⌘\). Under 640 px the rail folds too.
- Sessions keeps its tab strip for now, in the title row. M2 replaces it.

**What it buys.** The frame M2-M4 build on, and the polish you asked for, on every screen at once.

**What could go wrong, and how we'd know.**
- *A screen loses its title or actions in the move.* A rendered test per view checks that its title and actions
  land in the title row. The e2e opens every rail item.
- *The Workbench beside the browser breaks.* `workbench.spec.ts` sweeps 420, 520, 900 and 1280 px.
- *Window dragging stops working.* A rendered test checks the drag attribute on the title row's empty space and
  never on its controls. The click-through drags the window by hand.

**Calls you might disagree with.**
- The `Drawer` component is built here and used by the Board's task drawer. Changes adopts it in M2.
- The sidebar shortcut is ⌘\.

**Migrations:** none. **Needed from you:** nothing. This plan is part of the one integration PR
(`feat/app-shell-and-sessions`).

## Global constraints

- **Selenized only:** bg0, bg1, bg2, dim, fg0, fg1 and the accents, through Mantine's CSS variables
  (`theme.ts`). The rail sits on `--mantine-color-body` (bg0). The panel and title row sit on
  `--mantine-color-default` (bg1). The surface sits on bg0, with a 1 px `--mantine-color-default-border` (bg2) ring
  and a `0 1px 2px` shadow at `.35` in dark and `.06` in light, via `light-dark()`.
- **No new dependencies.** `@tanstack/react-virtual` and Mantine 9.8 are enough.
- **Keep every mount `AppLayout` has today:** `RenderLayout`, `ScreenRenderHost`, `ReconnectingBanner`,
  `useDesktopSync`, `useNewSessionKey`, and the first-run swap (`app-layout.tsx:146-177`, `320-325`).
- **Drag regions:** `data-tauri-drag-region` goes on the frame's empty boxes only, never on a control (sessions UI
  spec live check). A double-click on the title row's empty space zooms the window.
- **Every width rule is pure and table-tested** (§2). Components read a hook over that pure function.
- **Accessibility:** the rail is a `nav` named "Sections". A rail item's accessible name is the section's name, and
  its count goes in a separate phrase (`CountBadge`'s `phrase`). Tooltips aren't the only label.
- Code style and testing rules as in the repo. Exact pins. No `as` casts outside a validated boundary.

## Tasks

### Task 1: the pure rules

- `shell-layout.ts`, with a table test (`shell-layout.test.ts`). Each band, crossed with has-panel, hidden and
  overlay, is a named row:
  - wide + panel + not hidden → rail shown, panel column
  - wide + panel + hidden → rail shown, panel hidden
  - medium + panel → rail shown, panel overlay or hidden, by `overlayOpen`
  - narrow → rail and panel both overlay or hidden
  - no panel → panel `none` in every band
- `rail-items.ts`, with a table test of the order and its groups: global (Inbox, Factory, Sessions), the project
  tile, project sections (Board, Plan, UI Workbench, Knowledge), the foot (status, keep-awake, light/dark,
  Settings). The routes are today's (`router.tsx`).
- Prove each test red first. One commit.

### Task 2: the frame

1. Write `app-frame.tsx`: a CSS grid with columns rail 52 px, panel 260 px or 0, and the content; rows of 44 px and
   `1fr`.
2. `app-layout.tsx`'s `FullLayout` renders `AppFrame` in place of Mantine `AppShell`. Delete the burger, the
   fold queries, `TRAFFIC_LIGHTS_WIDTH`, `SESSIONS_STRIP_START` and `HeaderSlot` (keep the Sessions strip working:
   Task 3).
3. **The rail** (`rail.tsx`):
   - Items are 36 × 34 px, with an 18 px Tabler icon at stroke 1.6. The active item takes a bg1 fill (tone C).
   - Each has a `Tooltip position="right" openDelay={300}`.
   - Badges are compact `CountBadge`s pinned top-right: Inbox open, Sessions needing you (its click keeps today's
     go-to-next), Workbench waiting.
   - The divider is 24 px of bg2.
   - The project tile (26 px `ProjectTile`, 1 px ring) opens `ProjectSwitcher`'s dropdown to the right, which
     gains a **Project Settings** item.
   - At the foot: `AgentDownloadStatus`, `DaemonStatus`, `KeepAwakeControl`, `ColorSchemeToggle`, and Settings.
   - Keys: ↑/↓ move within the rail and Enter opens.
4. **The title row:**
   - The traffic lights stay at (16, 22).
   - The sidebar button (`IconLayoutSidebar`, "Hide the panel" / "Show the panel") sits at the panel's right end
     when wide, and just after the lights when medium or narrow.
   - The title row's content starts on the surface's inner left edge. It starts at 112 px when the rail is folded,
     or when medium with no panel column.
   - The 1 px bg2 "pipe" runs from 10 px down to the surface, at the panel/content edge.
5. **The surface:**
   - bg0, radius 10, inset 8 px (6 px narrow), with the ring and shadow above.
   - `ReconnectingBanner` sits at its top.
   - It keeps `display:flex; flex-direction:column; height` bounded to the window, as `AppShell.Main` did.
6. **⌘\:** bound in the frame. It toggles hidden (wide) or the overlay (medium, narrow).
   - The overlay is a panel-width sheet over the surface (narrow: rail plus panel). It's `inert` while closed, and
     Esc or a click outside closes it.
   - Hidden is kept in `localStorage` `tendb.shell.panel.<section>`, wrapped in try/catch.
7. **Rendered tests** (`app-frame.test.tsx`, through `renderWithDaemon`):
   - the rail's names, active item and badge phrases;
   - the project tile's menu with Project Settings;
   - ⌘\ in each band, with the store;
   - the drag attribute on empty boxes only;
   - the mounts still present (reconnecting banner, render mode).
8. Update `app.test.tsx`'s 13 tests that select the old navbar or burger. Commit.
