# Inbox M3: the Inbox on the panel, and M4: the Workbench queue in the panel

## Brief

**What changes for you.**
- The Inbox reads like mail. The panel lists the rows of one state, and the content shows the one you picked.
  - The panel's head holds search (⌘F), a filter menu and an `Open n · Waiting n · Resolved` switch.
  - Each row has two lines: the kind, title and age, then the project and a line for the state.
  - `j`/`k` move, `e` dismisses a flag from its row, and answering a card selects the next row.
- The tabs, the Waiting and Resolved tables and their pager go. All three states page as you scroll.
- The Open count in the switch, the rail and the menu bar comes from the daemon. A filter no longer changes it.
- A question shows the last three messages of its session. A check shows its diff, as today.
- **M4:** with a pass open, the UI Workbench's queue moves into the panel, and the pass's name and ⋯ go to its
  head. When the panel is folded or hidden, the strip on top comes back.

**What it buys.** The Inbox stays fast however much it holds, and its rows, counts and pages agree. That's the
2026-11-16 decision, built. One frame for every list.

**What could go wrong, and how we'd know.**
- *After a card comes or goes, a row shows twice or not at all.* A pure merge table covers every event, and a
  rendered test replays events against a page fetch in flight. A reconnect refetches the whole list, which heals it.
- *A count and the list disagree.* One grouping rule serves both. A real-SQLite table pins `total`, and a Slack
  change refreshes the counts too.
- *The first page is slow with thousands of open cards.* The e2e seeds 2,000 open flags, some grouped. It times
  Open's first row and one row lookup.
- *The keys stop answering.* `inbox-view.test.tsx`'s key tests move onto the panel, and none is deleted.

**Calls you might disagree with.**
1. **The daemon groups in its own code, not in SQL.** It reads the open cards' light columns and calls the pure
   rule in `packages/contracts`. A pass's Ready-page detail is a whole zod schema, which SQL would copy and drift
   from. The browser groups nothing, which amends the decision's "the desktop calls it".
2. **No migration and no index.** Search is `LIKE` on title and body, which folds ASCII case only. The keyset
   reads use `inbox_state_idx`. The index waits for six figures.
3. **A grouped row matches a search when any of its cards matches, and the whole row comes.**
4. **Kinds stay yellow in Open,** by DESIGN.md's rule, and gray in Waiting and Resolved. The mockup's blue ASK and
   green CHECK aren't used.
5. **A card triage hasn't placed shows in Open but isn't counted,** as today.
6. **Resolved's "when" filter is presets:** Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days. "Who answered" is the outcome filter.
7. **The title row reads "n open",** without the mockup's "across 2 projects", which would need a second count.
8. **10db's own cards** stay at the top of Open under a project filter, and hide under a kind filter or a search.
9. **A list you aren't looking at** is marked stale on an event, and reloads from its first page when shown again.
   Lists on screen are patched by row.
10. **(M4) With the panel shown,** the title row holds the page: its name, its state and its Take Out. Folded or
    hidden, today's pass header returns to the title row.
11. **Still to do:** notifications, Sessions' needing-you, the Board and the Workbench still read every open card
    (`GET /inbox`). That's a follow-up. Opening an Inbox item in a drawer from another view (shell §1.5) isn't
    built, because nothing opens one today.

**Migrations:** none. **Needed from you:** nothing.

## Global constraints

- Code style and testing rules as in the repo:
  - `as const` objects and branded ids;
  - zod at every boundary;
  - no `as` casts outside a validated boundary;
  - no non-null `!`. A missing id in a page is a typed error.
- **One grouping rule.** `inboxRowKey` in `packages/contracts/src/inbox-rows.ts` decides which cards share a row.
  The daemon calls it. Until Task 6b deletes it, the desktop's old `inboxRows` calls it too. After that, the
  desktop never groups: it places the rows the daemon sent.
- **The browser never loads a whole list for the Inbox.** The rows come from `GET /inbox/page` in pages of 50, read
  with `after`. Counts come from the `total` of a page-size-1 read. `GET /inbox` stays for its other readers only.
- **A list keeps its rows on screen** while a refetch, a filter or a search is on its way (`keepPreviousData`). It
  never returns to a loading state it has left (§1.4).
- **Rows are a fixed height and virtualised** (§4.1 "Performance"), with `@tanstack/react-virtual` and an overscan
  of 10. The next page loads within 10 rows of the end.
- **Selection follows a row's key.** When the selected row leaves, the row now at its index is selected.
- **The look is the spec's, cited by heading,** never copied. The panel's head and rows are as §1.3 The panel
  says, the Inbox row as §4.1 "A row" says, and the title row as §1.1 The title row says, as built in
  `shell/AGENTS.md`. The frame is being redesigned: draw through `Panel`, `TitleRow` and the theme's tokens, with
  no frame tones or sizes hard-coded in a view.
- **Page-number reads of `GET /inbox/page` keep working unchanged.** Sessions' chat tab uses them, and M3 never
  edits `views/sessions/**`.
- **A screen frame's panel host** (`screens/render-layout.tsx`) exists only while a section registers a panel. A
  picture of Board or a task then keeps its full width. Task 6c or Task 10 adds it, whichever lands first, and the
  other reuses it.
- **Copy is exact, as in §4.1:**
  - "Nothing needs you" (Open);
  - "Nothing is waiting on anyone" (Waiting);
  - "Nothing resolved yet" (Resolved);
  - "No matches", with Clear Filters, when a search or a filter is set.
- No new dependencies. Exact pins.

## Provider parity

The Inbox shows 10db's own records, so every feature reads the same on a Claude plan, on Claude through Bedrock
or an API key, and on a ChatGPT/Codex plan.

| Feature | Claude plan | Bedrock / API key | Codex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rows, grouping, counts, paging | 10db's cards; no provider call | same | same |
| Search and filters | SQL on 10db's cards | same | same |
| Live updates | 10db's event stream | same | same |
| A question's last three messages | 10db's stored `message` and `agent_message` items, written by the Claude adapter | same adapter | written by the Codex adapter as the same kinds |
| A check's diff | git (`useWorkstreamChanges`) | same | same |
| Waiting | Slack, never a provider | same | same |
| (M4) The queue in the panel | the pass's pages | same | same |

No feature refuses a provider.

## State and event table: the live list

"Loaded" means the pages in one list's cache. A list is **observed** while a mounted component reads it.
**Pending** means its first page hasn't landed yet. A lookup is `fetchRowOf` with that list's own filters, run in
that list's chain: one at a time, in event order, each waiting until the list's fetch is idle. **Test** names the
card whose test covers the row.

| # | State | Event | Outcome | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a card in a row of its own, selected | answered or dismissed here | `dropCard` on success, through the chain. The row at the same index is selected. The count refetches on the event | 5, 6b |
| 2 | any card loaded | answered elsewhere (`inbox.changed`, resolved) | the lookup answers null, and the card leaves. If it was selected, its draft goes and the row at its index is selected | 5, 6b |
| 3 | a grouped flag row ×n | Dismiss All | each card's event takes it out, and the row is looked up by a remaining card: ×n-1 … gone. The selection stays on the key while the row exists | 5, 6b |
| 4 | loaded, more pages | a new card sorting before `next` | inserted at its sort, in the page that holds it. The selection stays | 5 |
| 5 | loaded, more pages | a new card sorting after `next` | not inserted, since the next page brings it. Only the counts change | 5 |
| 6 | all pages loaded | a new card anywhere | inserted at its sort | 5 |
| 7 | a grouped row loaded | a card joins it (same flags, same pass) | the row comes back from the lookup, takes the loaded row's place, and is moved if its sort changed | 5 |
| 8 | a grouped row loaded | its first card leaves | the lookup answers null, or another key. Then the remaining card is looked up, and its row takes the new sort. Past `next`, the row leaves, and the next page brings it | 5 |
| 9 | an Open card | sent out while Slack is connected (`stakeholder_send`) | Open's lookup answers null, and it leaves. Waiting's lookup, if loaded, inserts it | 2, 3, 4, 5 |
| 10 | a Waiting card | returned, or Slack disconnects | the reverse of 9. A Slack connection change refetches Open and Waiting whole, and every count | 2, 3, 4, 5 |
| 11 | a flag loaded | its title or body changed | it leaves its old row, the old row is looked up by a remaining card, and its new row is inserted | 5 |
| 12 | a page fetch in flight, the first page of a pending list included | an event for that list | the lookup waits for the fetch to settle, then merges. Merging is idempotent by card and key | 5 |
| 13 | one list | two events for one card, close together | the chain runs them in order | 5 |
| 14 | a search or filter typed | a page of the old list lands | it lands in the old key's cache. The screen keeps the old rows until the new first page arrives. The new key is pending, so row 12 holds. The selection keeps its key if the new list has it, else takes the first row | 6a, 7 |
| 15 | the last loaded row selected, more pages | `j` or ↓ | `loadMore()`. A placeholder row shows, and the next real row is selected when it lands | 6b |
| 16 | as 15, already loading | `j` again | no second fetch. One move is applied when the page lands | 6b |
| 17 | the last row, no more pages | `j` or ↓ | nothing. The arrow keeps its default | 6b |
| 18 | scrolled within 10 rows of the end | — | `loadMore()` once while `loadingMore` | 6a |
| 19 | any | the socket reopens | `invalidateQueries()` refetches each loaded page from the first. Old rows stay up, and the selection follows its key, else its index | 5 |
| 20 | a lookup in flight | it fails | that list is invalidated: a whole refetch | 5 |
| 21 | a card being answered | 409 | a notice, "This card was already answered.", shows over the content. The card's lookup goes through the chain, and the row leaves. The next row is selected, and the notice stays until the selection moves again | 6b |
| 22 | Resolved loaded from its top | a card is resolved | inserted at the top, newest first. A tie on `updated_at` sorts by id, descending | 1, 5, 7 |
| 23 | a cached list not observed (the Inbox not shown, or another state) | any event | it is marked stale (`refetchType: 'none'`). When shown again it refetches from its first page, with its old rows up, at the same selection and scroll | 5, 6c |
| 24 | a system card selected | the check no longer finds it | it leaves, and the next row is selected | 6b |
| 25 | a screen frame (render mode) | any | every list, count and context read asks for the frame's project, or is allowed by the frame's token | 4, 5, 6c |
| 26 | `loadMore` in flight | it fails | the placeholder row says "Couldn't load more". The pending move is dropped, and the next `j` or near-end retries | 6a, 6b |
| 27 | a page fetch in flight | the builder answers a card on it | `dropCard` waits in the chain for the fetch, then drops the card, so the page can't bring it back | 5 |
