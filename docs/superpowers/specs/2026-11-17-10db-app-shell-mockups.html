3. Inbox: one pattern for Open, Waiting and Resolved

The panel lists the chosen state's rows, email-style. The rows are paged as you scroll and virtualised. Search and filters sit in the panel's head and run on the server. The content shows the selected item. j/k move the selection, Enter acts, e dismisses a flag from its row, and the next item takes its place. Waiting and Resolved work the same way, each with its own detail.
