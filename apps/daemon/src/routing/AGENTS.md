# apps/daemon/src/routing

- `routing/` is pure: flows, floors, AA's Intelligence Index, which every role is routed on (`decisions/2026-11-12-every-role-routes-on-intelligence.md`: AA retired its Coding and Agentic indexes), the router, escalation and step plans over rows its caller passes in. `choose-model.ts` (`chooseModel`) is the one place that picks a step's model and effort: persona pin, task setting, pattern (project's first), role setting (project's first), then Automatic, with tiers and prices from the profiles (`decisions/2026-11-07-one-function-chooses-a-steps-model.md`). `offeredProfiles(profiles, accountModels, provider)` narrows the profiles to the account's list; routing names no model, so a model is reached only through its tier, its price or a setting. Tiers run weakest first (`pools.ts` `TIERS`); a floor moves a step to Frontier, and a top pick from Capable spans Capable and Frontier (`topSpan`), so the split changes no pick. `retryStep` tries a retry-only pattern before `escalateStep`. A step with nothing routable holds a tier name as its `model`, no `profileId` and the setting that decided: the launcher must never launch it.
