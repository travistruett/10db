# apps/daemon/src/mcp

- `resize_task { rigor, why, opId }` (`mcp/tools.ts`, `setAgentRigor` in `packages/db`): a plan or build session sets its own task's rigor (source `agent`, reason `why`) and the next step boundary re-plans. Refusals are results (`resized: false`, `message`), never errors: any other step, a Workbench page (`task.passId`), a rigor the builder pinned (`rigorSource` builder), and a rigor below the kinds' minimum (`refuseRigorBelowKinds`). A success is recorded under its `opId` in the same write as the rigor, so a replay answers the first result and raises nothing; the notice (`InboxService.flag`, one option "Got it", `foldKey` `resize:<taskId>`, refreshed by a later resize) is a second write, so a crash between the two loses the notice while the card still shows the agent's reason.

- `report_pages` (`mcp/report-pages.ts`,
  `decisions/2026-11-10-report-pages-loads-only-a-page-areas-route.md`): the browser loads a reported route
  signed in, so `keptRoute` keeps only a page area's route, each parameter (`[id]`, `[...slug]`, `:id`) one
  `[A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,64}` value that is no page's literal segment at that depth, the agent's query dropped and the
  area route's own kept; at most four a turn (a pass page) or a run (a task outside a pass), refused in a
  read-only step, a separate copy, a pass page with no turn open or a project with no app address. Kept
  routes are `task_page_reports` rows, matched again by `keptRoute` (`pictures/kept-route.ts`) when the
  picture is taken: `pictureRoutes` adds the turn's to its turn picture, and to the after a pass page's last
  turn's or another task's run's. The brief of a pass page and
  of a pictured Your-folder task (`PromptInput.pictures`) names the tool.
- Shared areas in a pass (Workbench spec §7.2, §7.3, §12; `decisions/2026-11-09-a-shared-area-is-held-one-turn-and-lands-with-its-page.md`) are held one turn at a time. `request_claim` for a pass lane's shared area (`mcp/tools.ts`) passes `claimOrWait` a `SharedGrant`: `claimOrWait` keeps its synchronous look at the claim, so a held claim names its holder before `ClaimWaits.join` returns, and only a free claim goes to the async `grantSharedHold` (`pass/shared-chain.ts`), which first keeps an open hold on the area whose claim its lane lost, refuses (null) once the lane's turn has ended, then records the area's shared files that differ from the chain's tip (never a page's file, a page held as one, or a hazard a page holds: `sharedPathOf`) with their content in an unsigned commit, writes `acquireClaims` and `insertHold` in one write (a lost race writes nothing), then sets `refs/10db/passes/<pass>/holds/<claim>`. Before that, `askShared` (`pass/shared-ask.ts`; pure `sharedAskNeeded`) raises the builder's question when another task changed the area, by a change not dropped, or holds it; the live wait holds it and ends, withdrawing it, once the lane's turn has ended (step 2 of a turn's end withdraws it too); the slice timer has a third answer, `waiting: true` with no holder while the question is open, the answer kept under the opId when it comes between calls. A question a pass page's run asks, its own or the shared one, is raised without Park the task (`inbox/service.ts` adds it only to a question no pass holds the asker of: a page leaves its pass by Take Out); on a card raised with it before, Park the task answers a shared question as Keep It to This Page (`inbox/service.ts` never parks for one), a reply (which needs words) is written to the thread as a delivered builder message, or, once the turn has ended, an undelivered one handed to the runner (`McpDeps.deliverMessage`) so it starts a turn, and a question a restart left is withdrawn (`withdrawStaleClaimWaits`). `pass/shared-chain.ts`: `keepHold` keeps a hold's change as one unsigned commit on `refs/10db/passes/<pass>/shared` (`Git.chainCommit`, compare-and-swap, trailer `10db-hold`), numbered as its grant was, and in one write upserts the row, closes the hold, releases the claim and tells the other pages; every grant, keep and drop refuses with `PassFolderOffBranchError` while HEAD is off the pass's branch, and start-up leaves a hold open then; `closeHoldsOf` is step 2 of every turn end (`runner/pass-turn.ts`) and of a usage pause (the runner's `settleLane`); `sharedNeeded` and `landSharedChanges` (number order, one signed commit per change, `Git.landPaths`) are slice 7's sign-off seam. `pass/shared-drop.ts`: `dropShared` puts a change's files back to its hold's record, hashed again before each write, and appends a drop; refused while a later change touches its files or one changed on disk (an Abandon's `force` skips the refusals, never the hash); `sharedUnlanded` is `GET /passes/:passId/tasks/:taskId/take-out`, and the POST refuses a Drop it would not offer.
