# apps/daemon/src/accounts

- `accounts/` owns account pauses (`account_pause.<id>`; usage limit, monthly cap, refused), the monthly cap in local time on the injected clock, readiness and the preparer, staggered subscription starts, the account cards and the account service. The scheduler and the runner ask `AccountRuntime.blocked` before every session start; a cap or refusal pause lets running sessions finish and a usage limit stops them. `limits.ts` acts on a session's usage report: past a project's threshold its lanes hand off and it moves to its usable fallback (ready, not paused, capped when metered), or the builder is asked; at the hard limit a project with a fallback moves at once. Only projects with work in flight move or are asked.
- `accounts/` also moves projects between accounts. `PATCH /projects/:id` and the cards go through `AccountMoves.move` (`moves.ts`), which remembers a move off a usage-limited account; `move-back.ts` moves a project back by itself when the limit that moved it resets (`MoveCause.MoveBack`, a project event naming both accounts; a move the builder made by hand, or one recorded without `moveBackAutomatic`, raises the card instead); `usage-reports.ts` keeps each account's latest report in memory. `Runner.handOff` asks a project's lanes on an account to hand off in the account's own words (`HANDOFF_NUDGE_ACCOUNT`). The runner reads the project's account before each session and never resumes a conversation that ran on another account: `runner/next-action.ts` starts the lane fresh from its handoff.
