# apps/desktop/src/views/settings

- Settings (`views/settings/`) is one route over `sections.ts`. Each section has a `scope` (`SettingsScope`: This project or All projects; the sidebar's `SegmentedControl` shows one at a time) and a `subhead` its links group under. To fill a planned section, set its `planned` to null and replace its arm in `section-body.tsx`. `?focus=<name>` on a section's address scrolls to and focuses the element that called `useFocusTarget(name)` (`focus-target.ts`); `ConnectionStatus` (`connection-status.tsx`) is the teal, yellow or gray dot with words that every connection state uses.
- Settings › Network hosts (`views/settings/network-hosts-section.tsx`) lists, adds and removes a project's allowed hosts through `/projects/:id/network-hosts`; the daemon's refusal text is shown beside Add Host (`decisions/2026-10-29-project-network-hosts.md`).
- Settings › Trust (`views/settings/trust-section.tsx`, `trust-rows.ts`, `daemon/trust-queries.ts`) switches what comes along into every session through `/settings/trust`: one `Switch` per row of `TRUST_ROWS` (one per `TRUST_SWITCH_KEYS`, each with its note per agent and "Applies from the next session."), then Claude Code's installed plugins with their versions, a plugin off in Claude Code saying so until the builder switches it here, then a read-only Judge list: one row per account with the model a new task gets there, what judges its calls (`JUDGE_LABELS`), and the daemon's `why` for a rules row. A project's Trust (`project-trust-section.tsx`, `/projects/:id/trust`) gives each row and plugin As app / On / Off (As app removes the override), the project's Live systems (`/projects/:id/live-systems`, a `TagsInput` that sends each change at once and shows a refusal under the input, keeping the saved list), and a read-only Comes along list from the daemon's preview: what the project's next session would load, and each left-out item with its reason.
- Settings › Decisions (`views/settings/decisions-section.tsx`, `daemon/judge-queries.ts`) edits the judge's config and OpenRouter key through `/judge`. The key goes by `daemon/judge-secret.ts`, never TanStack Query, and the section shows only Set or Not set. The daemon emits no event for judge changes or calls, so the section invalidates its own queries after each mutation and polls the recent calls every 30 s. When `JudgeStatusDto.off` is true (`TENDB_JUDGE=off`) it says so and disables its controls.
- Settings › Models, under the AI subhead (`views/settings/models-section.tsx`, `model-drawer.tsx`, `daemon/model-queries.ts`) is one page that answers who each role gets first, and carries its own title. A single Account select (live accounts; the default account, else the first live one) drives everything, with a refresh `ActionIcon` the Account select's height (`size="input-xs"`, `aria-label="Refresh Models"`, a left tooltip) beside it that posts `/models/refresh`; with no live account the page says "Add an account to see what each role gets" and lists no rows, and a failed read of the account's models shows the daemon's message and Try Again. One dimmed `xs` line under the title says where the catalogue came from and how old it is (`GET /models/status`) and credits "Scores: Artificial Analysis" with a link (`artificial-analysis-credit.tsx`), once for the page. Top to bottom: "Who each role gets" (`role-picks-panel.tsx`, from `GET /models/picks?account=`: the router's choice per role at its starting need, with the global role settings and the role's default persona but no project settings, risk floors or budget ceilings, which its footnote says) with a violet dot per role, where a row selects the role (the chart then draws only its need line and ring, the other rows dim, a second click clears it) and a pick the chart does not draw says so in Why; "Intelligence and cost" (`rightsizing-chart.tsx`, `@mantine/charts` `ScatterChart`, log cost on X, Intelligence on Y, built by the pure `rightsizing-series.ts` and `pareto.ts`: every point gray, the frontier and its line blue, picks ringed violet, dashed labelled need lines, an opaque `bg_1` tooltip with a `bg_2` border, no legend and no tier colours; Every effort | Frontier only picks the dots); and "All models" (`models-table.tsx`, mantine-datatable): Model (the name on one line, then its status as dimmed text and New in blue), Tier (dimmed, filtered from its header), Intelligence, Cost per task, Used by (the roles whose pick it is, as plain text: violet marks picks only on the chart and in the roles table) and Steps run, opening sorted by Intelligence, highest first. The list is grouped by family (`model-families.ts`, pure): every Current model is a row, and a family's other versions are rows of the same table, indented, under its first Current model, behind an "N older" toggle ("Show 2 older versions of …" to a screen reader) that opens no drawer; a model with no maker or family is its own family, and an entry takes the place of its best-sorted row, so a cheap older version is never buried. Folded, a family's Used by also names, dimmed, the roles that run on a version under it. The list pages 25, 50 or 100 entries (25 to start) with the shared pager (`shell/table-pager.ts`, which Knowledge and the Plan record use too), counts entries rather than drawn rows, goes back to the first page when the filter, sort or account changes, and never shows a page past the last one. Model, Intelligence, Cost per task and Steps run sort, and the Model header searches names and model IDs. The account narrows the list to what it runs (`availableOn`) and prices the costs on it (`useModels(accountId)` keeps the previous rows up while it loads, and no ring is drawn on a price that is not this account's). A row, or Enter on its name button, opens `ModelDrawer`: the ID and tier, "What each effort buys" (the same chart of that model's efforts, ringed where a role uses it) with an effort table (Effort, Intelligence, Cost per task, Steps run, First pass), "Offered by" (each account with launch ID and own price, "Included in plan" for a plan), and the input and output price and speed as one dimmed line. There is no Columns menu: Input, Output and Speed live in the drawer. `model-order.ts` holds the order and the filters: a cell with no data and an Unknown model always sort last, and a dash, never 0, shows no data. A model is marked New when first seen in the last 7 days and more than a day after the earliest first-seen time of any model, so a fresh install's first batch is never New. No copy names a model. The desktop opener allows GitHub, OpenAI sign-in pages and `https://artificialanalysis.ai/*`, so the credit link opens Artificial Analysis's site in a browser tab and from the Tauri window alike (`decisions/2026-11-08-the-desktop-opener-opens-artificial-analysis.md`, `decisions/2026-11-13-the-models-page-answers-who-each-role-gets-first.md`). Under the catalogue line, `artificial-analysis-status-line.tsx` says where the scores come from and links to Settings › Integrations › Artificial Analysis (`?focus=artificial-analysis`).
- Model by role and patterns (`views/settings/role-settings.tsx`, `patterns-section.tsx`, `model-setting-select.tsx`, `daemon/model-settings-queries.ts`): Settings › Models by role holds the project's (key `team`) and, under All projects, the global ones (key `personas-and-flows`), and the task drawer's Edit tab has the task's own (shown when the board passes the project's `accountId`). Every one is the same `ModelSettingSelect`: Automatic, the three tiers, Top of tier, then the models of the relevant account (the project's at project level, every account at app level) grouped by tier, Unknown and Superseded ones marked and Retired and Not runnable ones left out (`isPickableModel` in `@10db/contracts`, the one rule the Plan picker, these dropdowns, the tier overrides and the daemon's `chooseModel` share). Every role offers a none choice that sends null (Use global in a project, 10db default in the global form) and a task offers No override. Team and Personas and flows are listed in the nav (`SettingsSection.built`) and keep their remaining planned work as a short note under the forms. A role saves the moment it is chosen; patterns are edited as a list and saved with Save patterns, in the order shown. A saved model no account offers any more stays listed as "(not offered)".
- Account model overrides (`views/settings/accounts/tier-overrides.tsx`, built): the account drawer's settings form (edit only; a new account has no list yet) ends with one field per tier, labelled `Efficient model`, `Balanced model`, `Capable model` and `Frontier model`, that saves `settings.tierOverrides` through the ordinary `PATCH /accounts/:id`. With a list (`GET /models/offers` has rows for the account) each is a dropdown of Automatic and the models that account offers, grouped by tier as the Models page groups them (`offeredModelGroups` in `model-setting-options.ts`, which leaves out what `isPickableModel` refuses); a saved override the account no longer offers stays listed as "(not offered)". Without one (a gateway that reports none) each is a text field for a model ID, and a tier's name typed there is refused (`tierOverrides` in the contracts). A blank or Automatic field sends no override. There is no model map and no offered-aliases field.
- Stakeholder questions (built; design in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-10db-stakeholder-questions-design.md`).
  `daemon/people.ts` holds every query for people, Slack, timers, routes and the send actions. Settings ›
  Integrations (`views/settings/integrations/`) has one card per integration: Slack's connects it (its manifest in
  `slack-manifest.ts`, which a repo test keeps equal to `docs/slack-app.md`), binds the builder to their Slack
  member ("Which one is you?", `which-is-you.tsx`) and keeps Change Who You Are and Disconnect (which asks first,
  `shell/confirm-modal.tsx`) in its menu. Settings › People (`views/settings/people/`) is only the people, with
  how 10db reaches each one, and the timers; Add Person never shows a handle (`person-handle.ts` makes one from
  the name and numbers a clash). Settings › Who Answers (`who-answers-section.tsx`) is the routing table. A question card shows what its
  people will get (`slack-preview.tsx`: the route line under the title with Change, the card's one reroute
  control; ⌘↵ sends, a bare Enter and the digits do nothing), Ask Someone (`ask-someone.tsx`), and, for someone
  not in Slack, the message with Copy for them beside it, from `InboxItemDto.copyFor`. Take Back posts in Slack,
  so it asks first (`take-back-modal.tsx`). The Inbox has an Open, a Waiting and a
  Resolved tab (`inbox-rows.ts` `splitInbox`: a sent, unreturned card is Waiting only while Slack is connected).
  `WorkstreamDto.waitingOn` draws "Waiting on Mark in Slack · 3h" on a card that keeps its plain border. A popover
  that holds a Select draws the Select's list inside itself (`comboboxProps={{ withinPortal: false }}`), or a click
  on an option closes it. `e2e/stakeholder-questions.spec.ts` seeds its cards with `apps/daemon/scripts/seed-*.ts`.
