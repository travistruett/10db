# apps/desktop/src/views/settings

- Settings (`views/settings/`) is one route over `sections.ts`. Each section has a `scope` (`SettingsScope`: This project or All projects; the sidebar's `SegmentedControl` shows one at a time) and a `subhead` its links group under. To fill a planned section, set its `planned` to null and replace its arm in `section-body.tsx`. `?focus=<name>` on a section's address scrolls to and focuses the element that called `useFocusTarget(name)` (`focus-target.ts`); `ConnectionStatus` (`connection-status.tsx`) is the teal, yellow or gray dot with words that every connection state uses.
- Settings › Network hosts (`views/settings/network-hosts-section.tsx`) lists, adds and removes a project's allowed hosts through `/projects/:id/network-hosts`; the daemon's refusal text is shown beside Add Host (`decisions/2026-10-29-project-network-hosts.md`).
- Settings › Trust (`views/settings/trust-section.tsx`, `trust-rows.ts`, `daemon/trust-queries.ts`) switches what comes along into every session through `/settings/trust`: one `Switch` per row of `TRUST_ROWS` (one per `TRUST_SWITCH_KEYS`, each with its note per agent and "Applies from the next session."), then Claude Code's installed plugins with their versions, a plugin off in Claude Code saying so until the builder switches it here, then a read-only Judge list: one row per account with the model a new task gets there, what judges its calls (`JUDGE_LABELS`), and the daemon's `why` for a rules row. A project's Trust (`project-trust-section.tsx`, `/projects/:id/trust`) gives each row and plugin As app / On / Off (As app removes the override), the project's Live systems (`/projects/:id/live-systems`, a `TagsInput` that sends each change at once and shows a refusal under the input, keeping the saved list), and a read-only Comes along list from the daemon's preview: what the project's next session would load, and each left-out item with its reason.
- Settings › Decisions (`views/settings/decisions-section.tsx`, `daemon/judge-queries.ts`) edits the judge's config and OpenRouter key through `/judge`. The key goes by `daemon/judge-secret.ts`, never TanStack Query, and the page shows only Set or Not set. The daemon emits no event for judge changes or calls, so the page invalidates its own queries after each mutation and polls the stats (`useDecisionStats`, `GET /judge/decisions`, the last 30 days) and the calls (`useJudgeCallsPage`, `GET /judge/calls/page`) every 30 s. Top to bottom: the intro line; the master switch ("Fast-model decisions") with Paid by under it; one grouped table of every decision (`decisions-table.tsx`: Decision, Status, Model, Sure enough, Last 30 days, under group header rows); Recent decisions. `decision-catalogue.ts` is the one place a decision is added: its group, label, plain-words copy, mode (Acting or Shadow) and the clause Recent decisions says when the call fell back; a decision the daemon configures but the catalogue lacks fails the catalogue's test, and a group with no rows is not drawn. `decision-numbers.ts` holds the pure text: status (Acting, Shadow, Off, and Off (all decisions) for every row while the master switch is off or `TENDB_JUDGE=off`), the Last 30 days line (a decision the daemon leaves out of the stats reads "No calls"; the dash is only for stats that could not be read), the threshold preview and the outcome words. Paid by (`paid-by.tsx`, focus target `paid-by`) is the OpenRouter account's select and key state; with no usable key every status is followed by "· no key" and every call falls back to today's rule. A row, or Enter on its name, opens `decision-drawer.tsx`: what it asks and does, its switch, `decision-model-picker.tsx` (from `GET /judge/models`, grouped by maker, each with its price; one 10db can't call is disabled with the reason, and so noted when saved; the daemon saves a pick only after it answers a test decision, so the field shows the pick while it is asked and the saved model again with the refusal; with no key it asks for an account under Paid by), Sure enough (`sure-enough.tsx`: a 50 to 99% slider whose edit is kept until saved, saved once after the builder stops, flushed on blur and on unmount, never on a click that doesn't move it; a floor saved outside 50 to 99 is drawn at the nearest end with its saved value in words; the preview counts the last 30 days' answers a floor would have acted on), the decision's last 30 days and its own Recent decisions. Recent decisions (`recent-decisions.tsx`) is a fixed-height table the daemon pages and filters by decision and outcome from its headers; a filter change goes back to page 1, and a filter that matches nothing keeps the header. When `JudgeStatusDto.off` is true it says so and disables the controls; the numbers and calls still show. Plan's Sizing and Questions measures are in Plan's view, not here.
- Settings › Models, under the AI subhead (`views/settings/models-section.tsx`, `model-drawer.tsx`, `daemon/model-queries.ts`) is one page that opens on the playbook and the kinds, and carries its own title. A single Account select (live accounts; the default account, else the first live one) drives everything, with a refresh `ActionIcon` the Account select's height (`size="input-xs"`, `aria-label="Refresh Models"`, a left tooltip) beside it that posts `/models/refresh`; with no live account the page says "Add an account to see what each role gets" and lists no rows, and a failed read of the account's models shows the daemon's message and Try Again. One dimmed `xs` line under the title says where the catalogue came from and how old it is (`GET /models/status`) and credits "Scores: Artificial Analysis" with a link (`artificial-analysis-credit.tsx`), once for the page. Top to bottom: the move report (`move-report-note.tsx`, `GET /playbook/move-report`: the settings the move to the playbook could not carry over, shown until Dismiss deletes it, nothing when empty); "Playbook" (`playbook-table.tsx` in a `RaisedPanel`, `daemon/playbook-queries.ts`): a row per rigor (Light, Standard, Careful, Critical) and a column per role, where a cell shows the model and effort it resolves to on the page's account, and under it its setting (`settingSummary`: "Auto, at least 40", a tier, "Top of Capable"), and a role the row does not run is blank. The resolved pick is the daemon's own (`GET /models/picks?account=&rigor=&project=`, one query per rigor, `useEveryRigorPicks`); the UI never re-implements routing. A cell opens a popover (`playbook-cell-editor.tsx`, fields in `playbook-cell-fields.tsx`: Persona, Model with a minimum for Automatic and a tier for Top of tier, Effort, the resolved pick with its cost, Reset). A field saves at once and writes this scope’s own fields only (`ownCell` in `playbook-edits.ts`: a field set by the scope above or by 10db’s default is sent as null, so editing one field never copies an inherited value into the scope); the none choice reads "10db default" globally and "Use global" in a project. Fields inside a popover keep their lists in it (`withinPortal: false`), or choosing from a list would count as a click outside and close it. A row’s name opens `playbook-row-drawer.tsx`: a checkbox per step with Build fixed on and a plan review needing the plan (`toggleStep`); Reset hands the row back. "Kinds" (`kinds-section.tsx`, `kind-drawer.tsx`, `daemon/kind-queries.ts`): a mantine-datatable list in the builder’s order with Move Up and Down arrows (`PUT /kinds/order`) and Add Kind; a row opens the drawer, which edits label, description, minimum rigor, the four switches, added steps and one override per role (persona, model, effort; a role with nothing set is not stored) and saves every field except the key, which is typed once on Add Kind and read-only after; Delete Kind asks first. `settings.changed` events for `playbook` and `kind` refetch both lists and the picks. "Intelligence and cost" (`rightsizing-chart.tsx`, `@mantine/charts` `ScatterChart`, log cost on X, Intelligence on Y, built by the pure `rightsizing-series.ts` and `pareto.ts`: every point gray, the frontier and its line blue, picks ringed violet, dashed labelled need lines, an opaque `bg_1` tooltip with a `bg_2` border, no legend and no tier colours; Every effort | Frontier only picks the dots, and a Rings select picks the rigor whose picks are ringed, Standard first); and "All models" (`models-table.tsx`, mantine-datatable): Model (the name on one line, then its status as dimmed text and New in blue), Tier (dimmed, filtered from its header), Intelligence, Cost per task, Used by (the roles whose pick is it at the rigor the chart shows, as plain text: violet marks picks only on the chart) and Steps run, opening sorted by Intelligence, highest first. The list is grouped by family (`model-families.ts`, pure): every Current model is a row, and a family's other versions are rows of the same table, indented, under its first Current model, behind an "N older" toggle ("Show 2 older versions of …" to a screen reader) that opens no drawer; a model with no maker or family is its own family, and an entry takes the place of its best-sorted row, so a cheap older version is never buried. Folded, a family's Used by also names, dimmed, the roles that run on a version under it. The list pages 25, 50 or 100 entries (25 to start) with the shared pager (`shell/table-pager.ts`, which Knowledge and the Plan record use too), counts entries rather than drawn rows, goes back to the first page when the filter, sort or account changes, and never shows a page past the last one. Model, Intelligence, Cost per task and Steps run sort, and the Model header searches names and model IDs. The account narrows the list to what it runs (`availableOn`) and prices the costs on it (`useModels(accountId)` keeps the previous rows up while it loads, and no ring is drawn on a price that is not this account's). A row, or Enter on its name button, opens `ModelDrawer`: the ID and tier, "What each effort buys" (the same chart of that model's efforts, ringed where a role uses it) with an effort table (Effort, Intelligence, Cost per task, Steps run, First pass), "Offered by" (each account with launch ID and own price, "Included in plan" for a plan), and the input and output price and speed as one dimmed line. There is no Columns menu: Input, Output and Speed live in the drawer. `model-order.ts` holds the order and the filters: a cell with no data and an Unknown model always sort last, and a dash, never 0, shows no data. A model is marked New when first seen in the last 7 days and more than a day after the earliest first-seen time of any model, so a fresh install's first batch is never New. No copy names a model. The desktop opener allows GitHub, OpenAI sign-in pages and `https://artificialanalysis.ai/*`, so the credit link opens Artificial Analysis's site in a browser tab and from the Tauri window alike (`decisions/2026-11-08-the-desktop-opener-opens-artificial-analysis.md`, `decisions/2026-11-13-the-models-page-answers-who-each-role-gets-first.md`). Under the catalogue line, `artificial-analysis-status-line.tsx` says where the scores come from and links to Settings › Integrations › Artificial Analysis (`?focus=artificial-analysis`).
- Model setting select (`views/settings/model-setting-select.tsx`, `model-setting-options.ts`): one `ModelSettingSelect` is used wherever a model is chosen (a playbook cell, a kind’s override, the task drawer’s Edit tab, shown there when the board passes the project’s `accountId`): Automatic, the three tiers, Top of tier, then the models of the relevant account (the project’s at project level, every account at app level) grouped by tier, Unknown and Superseded ones marked and Retired and Not runnable ones left out (`isPickableModel` in `@10db/contracts`, the one rule the Plan picker, these dropdowns, the tier overrides and the daemon’s `chooseModel` share). A none choice sends null. A saved model no account offers any more stays listed as "(not offered)". Settings › Models by role (key `team`, project scope) is that project’s playbook (`project-playbook.tsx`): a Default rigor select (`PATCH /projects/:id` `defaultRigor`) and the same table with Use global; its remaining planned work is a short note under it. The app-wide `personas-and-flows` section is gone and its address lands on Models (`sectionForAddress`). The old role settings and patterns forms and their routes are gone; the old tables are read only by the daemon's move to the playbook.
- Account model overrides (`views/settings/accounts/tier-overrides.tsx`, built): the account drawer's settings form (edit only; a new account has no list yet) ends with one field per tier, labelled `Efficient model`, `Balanced model`, `Capable model` and `Frontier model`, that saves `settings.tierOverrides` through the ordinary `PATCH /accounts/:id`. With a list (`GET /models/offers` has rows for the account) each is a dropdown of Automatic and the models that account offers, grouped by tier as the Models page groups them (`offeredModelGroups` in `model-setting-options.ts`, which leaves out what `isPickableModel` refuses); a saved override the account no longer offers stays listed as "(not offered)". Without one (a gateway that reports none) each is a text field for a model ID, and a tier's name typed there is refused (`tierOverrides` in the contracts). A blank or Automatic field sends no override. There is no model map and no offered-aliases field.
- Stakeholder questions (built; design in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-10db-stakeholder-questions-design.md`).
  `daemon/people.ts` holds every query for people, Slack, timers, routes and the send actions. Settings ›
  Integrations (`views/settings/integrations/`) has one card per integration: Slack's connects it (its manifest in
  `slack-manifest.ts`, which a repo test keeps equal to `docs/slack-app.md`), binds the builder to their Slack
  member ("Which one is you?", `which-is-you.tsx`) and keeps Change Who You Are and Disconnect (which asks first,
  `shell/confirm-modal.tsx`) in its menu. Settings › People (`views/settings/people/`) is only the people, with
  how 10db reaches each one, and the timers; Add Person never shows a handle (`person-handle.ts` makes one from
  the name and numbers a clash). Settings › Who Answers (`who-answers-section.tsx`) is the routing table. A question card shows what its
  people will get (`slack-preview.tsx`: the route line under the title with Change, the card's one reroute
  control; ⌘↵ sends, a bare Enter and the digits do nothing), Ask Someone (`ask-someone.tsx`), and, for someone
  not in Slack, the message with Copy for them beside it, from `InboxItemDto.copyFor`. Take Back posts in Slack,
  so it asks first (`take-back-modal.tsx`). The Inbox has an Open, a Waiting and a
  Resolved tab (`inbox-rows.ts` `splitInbox`: a sent, unreturned card is Waiting only while Slack is connected).
  `WorkstreamDto.waitingOn` draws "Waiting on Mark in Slack · 3h" on a card that keeps its plain border. A popover
  that holds a Select draws the Select's list inside itself (`comboboxProps={{ withinPortal: false }}`), or a click
  on an option closes it. `e2e/stakeholder-questions.spec.ts` seeds its cards with `apps/daemon/scripts/seed-*.ts`.
