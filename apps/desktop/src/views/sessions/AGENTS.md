# apps/desktop/src/views/sessions

- Sessions (`/sessions`; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-10db-sessions-simplified-design.md` §6, shell spec `2026-11-17-10db-app-shell-design.md`) is a global screen of sessions: chats with no tab strip. `SessionsView` (`sessions-view.tsx`) takes `?chat=<ChatId>` or `?fresh=<id>` (`sessions-search.ts`), reads the open chats (`daemon/chat-queries.ts`) and the fresh tabs the window keeps in `localStorage` (`fresh-tabs.ts`), draws `SessionsPanel` into the shell's panel (`sessions-panel.tsx`: head with search and New Session, projects in the project list's order with a 14 px tile, 32 px rows from `session-row.tsx`, Recently closed (five, `tendb.sessions.closed`) at the foot) and the selected session or fresh tab in the content. `panel-order.ts` is the one order the panel, ⌘1-9, ⌘⇧[ ], ⌃Tab and ⌘J walk (`panelGroups`, `panelOrder`; `topPosition` is where a new session goes, the top of its project; `dropTarget` is what a drag asks of the daemon). A row's icon (reading, here, branch, terminal) and one mark (spinner, needs you, error, paused, pull request, merged; the spinner is a still dot under reduced motion) come from `row-state.ts`, over `tab-info.ts`'s `chatInfo` (`tab-state.ts`: a card to answer, an error, paused, working, in the terminal, a new reply). A pull request's mark is drawn for the selected session only. Past 200 rows `@tanstack/react-virtual` draws the rows in view and drag is off. Search is `GET /chats/search` (`useSearchChats`, the old rows kept while the next is read). With no session open a fresh tab opens, so the screen is never blank. The views here never compare a harness id (ESLint, as in the kit); the agent's name is `capabilities.label`.
- A fresh tab (`fresh-tab.tsx`) is not a chat until its first `@` or its first send; then exactly one chat is made in the chosen project (`useCreateChat`, one at a time even when an `@` and a send come together), the tab is replaced by it, and its draft moves to the chat's (`session-drafts.ts`: a module store, so a tab switch, a closed tab that returns and a visit to another screen keep the words). A send asked for travels as a request the chat's tab takes once, so a failed post leaves the draft in the chat and the next send goes to the same conversation. `GET /projects/:id/chat-models` says `unavailable` for a ChatGPT-plan project: its words show, Recent and Start from stay, the composer is off.
- A session's title row is `session-title.tsx` drawn into `TitleRow`: the title (double-click renames), the ⌄ switcher (`session-switcher.tsx`: open sessions by project, Recently closed, New Session ⌘T), the meta line (`session-meta.ts`: where the session works, what changed and where the change has got to, in git's words), the agent and its model (dim, from 992 px), the context ring, the git step button, the terminal button and ⋯ (`session-menu.tsx`, the items the panel's right-click menu shares: Rename, File as Tasks, Open in Terminal, Make a Board Task…, Archive, Close). Make a Board Task… opens `/board?newTask=<title>`, which the Board reads once into New Task. `ChatTab` (`chat-tab.tsx`) is the conversation in the kit's `ConversationLayout` column (`chatEntries` over the kit's `itemEntries`: a chat tool call on 10db's server whose result parses as `ChatToolResult` and agrees with its tool becomes a `TaskRow`, with Open on Board and Undo; a judged denial is the kit's own row with Allow This, sending "Go ahead: {title}"), the cards it waits on (`PermissionRequest`) and the composer, whose bottom row holds Attach, Dictate (desktop shell only) and the model chip (`conversation/model-menu.tsx`: `PATCH /chats/:id` with `model` and `effort`, applied from the next turn, off while a turn runs or waits). A fresh tab keeps its model and effort in `fresh-tabs.ts` and creates the chat with both, at `topPosition`. Enter sends and Shift+Enter is a new line; Enter picks from an open `@` or `/` list. A project removed from 10db leaves the transcript and "This project was removed from 10db." with no composer.
- Where a session works (spec §1-3): `ChatDto.work` says reading (null), choosing, here, moving or branch, and `GET /chats/:id/work` (`daemon/chat-work-queries.ts`, refetched on chat, project and turn-end events and every 2 minutes with a pull request open) says what changed. While choosing, `work-bar.tsx` sits above the composer: "{agent} is about to start changing files.", Here, on `<branch>` and On a New Branch (`POST /chats/:id/work`; a 409 says where the session works now), the uncommitted-files line and the holder's line; 1 and 2 choose, Esc hides it until `work.askedAt` changes, and it takes the focus unless the composer has words in it. While moving, the composer reads "Moving to `<branch>`…".
- The git step (`git-step-button.tsx`, in the title row's `steps`): one button for `ChatWorkStateDto.next`: Commit… (`commit-modal.tsx`: the message starts as the title, the files in `touched` start checked, the rest listed unchecked, `commit-paths.ts`), Open Pull Request, Merge (disabled with "Checks running · 3/9" while they run, `checksWords`) and Merge into `<base>`. The steps are `daemon/chat-git-queries.ts`; each answers the new `ChatWorkStateDto`, which the work read takes. While the session's own turn runs a step reads "Waits for the turn to end". A failed step is a dismissible `role="alert"` under the title row. Archive posts without a body; a 409 carries what would be lost (`ArchivePreviewDto`), `archive-confirm.tsx` lists it, and Archive and Delete These Files sends those lists back, so files that appeared since ask again (`use-session-closing.ts`). Commit's file list comes from the changes read, so committed files on a branch are listed too.
- Keys are a pure judge (`session-keys.ts`) heard by `useSessionKeys` on Sessions and, for ⌘T alone, by the shell's `useNewSessionKey`; Esc stays the chat tab's own stop; ⌘⇧↩ is free. Closing patches `tabPosition: null` and pushes the session, its place and its title onto the closed stack (`closed-tabs.ts`, five, `localStorage`); ⌘⇧T, Recently closed and Open Session take from it (`use-session-closing.ts`), and an archived chat is unarchived first. A chat's draft text and mentions persist in `tendb.sessions.drafts` (pictures do not). A fresh tab the shell wrote for ⌘T is read in when the address names it, except one this screen has just dropped.
- Notifications are `desktop/notify-chat.ts`: "{title} replied" (a chat going from working to idle, when Settings › Sessions allows it) and "{title} needs you" for a chat's card, each only while the window is not focused, and a click opens the tab. The announcer's words are `session-announcements.ts`.
- Your folder: `useProjectFolder` reads `GET /projects/:id/folder`, refetched on `project`, `workstream` and `pass` events and every 15 s (a terminal's branch switch sends no event). `folder-holders.ts`'s `keepingHolders` names the holders that keep this chat's turn from writing. A blocked Here send waits: `folder-wait.tsx` reads "{holder} is changing files on `main`." ("{a} and {b} are changing files…" with several holders) and "Your message runs once it's done." and offers Stop only. While the chat waits, a send passes `wait`, since it would cancel the wait.
- A chat's footer shows the agent's plan just above the working line (`conversation/todo-list.tsx`: the transcript's latest `plan` item as "Plan · 3 of 7 done · Now: …", open while work remains, folded once every step is done, reopened by a new list, an unfinished step of an ended turn read as interrupted; hidden without `capabilities.plan`) and its background tasks above the composer (`conversation/background-tasks.tsx`: `conversation.backgroundTasks`, the open session's running commands, monitors and subagents; foldable). Each scrolls inside a height cap. An agent with `capabilities.backgroundTasks` false (Codex) gets one dim line saying so, never an empty panel.
- A sealed chat (`conversation.state === 'sealed'`) has no composer: its line (`HandedOffLine` in `chat-tab.tsx`) says where the work went, with Open Task, which opens the task's drawer on the Board (`useOpenTask`, `/board?task=`); its transcript's `handed_to` notice is a divider with Open. There are no task tabs: `/sessions?task=<id>` redirects to `/board?task=<id>` in the route, and a closed-tab store entry for a task is dropped when read.
- A pass refused only because chats' turns hold Your folder offers **Stop the Turn and Open** and **Open When It Ends** (`when-busy.ts`; the Workbench's Start Pass reads `whenBusy` from `useProjectFolder`). An open that waits answers `pending`: `FolderDto.pendingOpen` draws `pending-open-line.tsx` on the Workbench.
- Changes (`changes-drawer.tsx`, `changes-panel.tsx`, `changes-files.ts`; ⌘⇧D is `changes` in `session-keys.ts`, heard by the selected `ChatTab`): `GET /chats/:id/changes` (`daemon/chat-changes-queries.ts`, under the work's key so its events refetch it), with the last diff kept on screen while the newer one is read. The title row's "Changes +41 −6" button (shown once anything changed, or when the read fails and a pull request has the changes) and ⌘⇧D toggle a `SurfaceDrawer`; the builder's choice is kept per session (`changes-choice.ts`, for the window), and it never opens by itself. At 1200 px of session width the conversation column gives the drawer's share up and sits left of it; under that the drawer covers it. `parseChanges` cuts the unified diff into files and hunks, each drawn by the kit's `HunkDiff`, whose line numbers are buttons that put `@path:line` and the line, quoted, into the session's composer (`withQuote`). Not built: the split view, the check line under the header, and the base's name.
- Open in Terminal (sessions UI spec §10): `terminal-button.tsx` (the title row's `terminal-2` icon, shown with `capabilities.takeover` and not on a sealed chat or one already in the terminal) opens `terminal-popover.tsx`, which reads `GET …/takeover/preview` for the daemon's notes and refusal and confirms with Enter. `ChatTab` shows `terminal-banner.tsx` while the conversation is `taken_over` (Hand Back behind its question, Copy Command through `POST …/takeover/command`, focus on Hand Back), the meta line says "in your terminal since {HH:mm}", the composer reads "Held until you hand back…" and each unsent message shows "Held until you hand back". Archiving such a chat asks "End takeover and archive?" in `sessions-view.tsx`. Settings › Sessions saves the terminal choice through `PATCH /settings/sessions` (`daemon/sessions-settings-queries.ts`). The copy says zsh or bash for the pasted line, never fish. A `taken_over` chat's tab is its own state (`in_terminal`: a dimmed `terminal-2`, a dimmed title, "in your terminal" spoken and in the Recent line). A clipboard that refuses the command leaves it in the banner to copy by hand (the popover is gone by then).
