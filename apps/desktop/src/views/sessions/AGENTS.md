# apps/desktop/src/views/sessions

- Sessions (`/sessions`, sidebar item third after Inbox and Factory; UI spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-10db-sessions-ui.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-10db-sessions-m5a-sessions.md`) is a global screen whose tabs are chats. `SessionsView` (`sessions-view.tsx`) takes `?chat=<ChatId>` or `?fresh=<id>` (`sessions-search.ts`), reads the open chats (`daemon/chat-queries.ts`) and the fresh tabs the window keeps in `localStorage` (`fresh-tabs.ts`: pure functions over the list), draws the strip through `HeaderSlot` (`shell/header-slot.tsx`; the shell keeps the drag region on its own boxes and the slot's tabs are not part of it; on Sessions the slot starts at 88 px) and the selected tab beneath it. With no tab it opens a fresh one, so the screen is never blank. The views here never compare a harness id (ESLint, as in the kit); the agent's name is `capabilities.label`.
- A fresh tab (`fresh-tab.tsx`) is not a chat until its first `@` or its first send; then exactly one chat is made in the chosen project (`useCreateChat`, one at a time even when an `@` and a send come together), the tab is replaced by it, and its draft moves to the chat's (`session-drafts.ts`: a module store, so a tab switch, a closed tab that returns and a visit to another screen keep the words). A send asked for travels as a request the chat's tab takes once, so a failed post leaves the draft in the chat and the next send goes to the same conversation. `GET /projects/:id/chat-models` says `unavailable` for a ChatGPT-plan project: its words show, Recent and Start from stay, the composer is off.
- A chat's tab (`chat-tab.tsx`) is the session bar (`session-bar.tsx`: title edited in place, the kind line from `kind-line.ts`, the agent's name, File as Tasks, the ⋯ menu; the model chip and the context ring are the composer's, below), the conversation in the kit's `ConversationLayout` column (`chatEntries` over the kit's `itemEntries`: a chat tool call on 10db's server whose result parses as `ChatToolResult` and agrees with its tool becomes a `TaskRow`, with Open on Board and Undo; a judged denial is the kit's own row with Allow This, sending "Go ahead: {title}"), the cards it waits on (`PermissionRequest`) and the composer, whose bottom row holds Attach, Dictate (desktop shell only), the model chip (`conversation/model-menu.tsx`: the account's models, an Effort submenu with only the levels the model in use lists in the models list, More models with search; `PATCH /chats/:id` with `model` and `effort`, applied from the next turn, off while a turn runs or waits, the daemon's refusal beside it) and the context ring (`conversation/context-ring.tsx`, `ConversationDto.contextPct`, the same for Claude and Codex sessions: both adapters emit `context` events; the percent and, on a metered account, the cost are in its tooltip). A fresh tab keeps its model and effort in `fresh-tabs.ts` and creates the chat with both. Enter sends and Shift+Enter is a new line; Enter picks from an open `@` or `/` list. A project removed from 10db leaves the transcript and "This project was removed from 10db." with no composer.
- The strip (`tab-strip.tsx`, `session-tab.tsx`, `all-tabs-menu.tsx`) shows one state per tab from `tab-state.ts` (`tabState` takes the first that holds: a card to answer, an error, paused, working, new reply, idle; `needsYou` is the first two). The open Inbox cards are the only source of "needs you" (`needs-you.ts` groups them by `detail.conversationId`); the tab's ring, the sidebar badge (`needing-you-count.ts`, whose click goes to the next such tab from any view) and the Inbox card's Open Session (`useOpenSession`, in `open-session.ts`) all read it, and nothing is stored. A tab is seen while selected with the window focused (`seen.ts`, `localStorage`). `strip-layout.ts` says full from 900 px, compact from 640 (an unselected tab is its tile and state; the session bar's model and meter are read in ⋯, not changed) and a switcher under 640 (`AllTabsMenu` with its `switcher` prop; the tabpanel is labelled by a hidden copy of the tab's name).
- Keys are a pure judge (`session-keys.ts`) heard by `useSessionKeys` on Sessions and, for ⌘T alone, by the shell's `useNewSessionKey`; Esc stays the chat tab's own stop. Closing patches `tabPosition: null` and pushes the tab, its place and its title onto the closed stack (`closed-tabs.ts`, five, `localStorage`); ⌘⇧T, All Tabs' Recently Closed and Open Session take from it (`use-tab-closing.ts`), and an archived chat is unarchived first. A chat's draft text and mentions persist in `tendb.sessions.drafts` (pictures do not). A fresh tab the shell wrote for ⌘T is read in when the address names it, except one this screen has just dropped.
- Notifications are `desktop/notify-chat.ts`: "{title} replied" (a chat going from working to idle, when Settings › Sessions allows it) and "{title} needs you" for a chat's card, each only while the window is not focused, and a click opens the tab. The announcer's words are `session-announcements.ts`.
- Your folder (sessions spec §7.3): `useProjectFolder` reads `GET /projects/:id/folder`, refetched on `project`,
  `workstream` and `pass` events and every 15 s (a terminal's branch switch sends no event; `conversation` events do
  not refetch it, since each read runs git). `kind-line.ts` words the bar from it (`keepingWords` is the holders that
  keep this chat out, which also drive the composer's choices; the daemon's backticks are stripped). Every send that
  can run while the folder is held asks first: a chat's Send, a fresh tab's first message (the choice travels in the
  send request to the chat made from it; File as Tasks is offered only where a conversation exists) and Allow This
  (which puts "Go ahead" in the composer). `folder-wait.tsx` is the row for a message that waits; it follows the
  chat's own `waiting_chat` holder, so it stays while the message is parked even when nothing still keeps the chat
  out. While the chat waits, a send that names no folder passes `wait`, since it would cancel the wait. Send Now
  Reading Only is `POST /chats/:id/stop-waiting`, harmless when nothing waits any more. Before the folder is read the
  line is "Chat · reads {project}".
- A chat's footer shows the agent's plan just above the working line (`conversation/todo-list.tsx`: the transcript's latest `plan` item as "Plan · 3 of 7 done · Now: …", open while work remains, folded once every step is done, reopened by a new list, an unfinished step of an ended turn read as interrupted; hidden without `capabilities.plan`) and its background tasks above the composer (`conversation/background-tasks.tsx`: `conversation.backgroundTasks`, the open session's running commands, monitors and subagents; foldable). Each scrolls inside a height cap. An agent with `capabilities.backgroundTasks` false (Codex) gets one dim line saying so, never an empty panel.
- Do It (UI spec §8; plan `2026-11-16-10db-sessions-m6b.md`): the session bar's **Do It…** is a `SplitButton` (File as Tasks is in its menu; ⌘⇧↩ is `do_it` in `session-keys.ts`, heard by the chat's tab). Its sheet (`do-it-sheet.tsx`, words in `do-it-words.ts`) reads `GET /chats/:id/do-it` (`daemon/do-it-queries.ts`, under the chats' key so a `chat` event refetches it; read again every few seconds only while the agent writes the brief) and asks `POST /chats/:id/brief` once when it opens. The title and brief follow the daemon's until the builder types in that field (typing stops nothing on the daemon); Cancel and the close button drop a brief still being written (`DELETE /chats/:id/brief`). A place the daemon refuses stays in the list, disabled, with its words and link; the sheet falls to the other place when only it is offered. A submit refusal is shown beside Do It and keeps the brief. The fourth choice, Do It in a Separate Copy, is `ComposerHeld.onDoItSeparately`: it opens the sheet on that place with the unsent draft in the brief request.
- A task Do It made has a tab of its own (`task-tabs.ts`: the window's list in `localStorage` `tendb.sessions.tasks`, with a store like `closed-tabs.ts` so the Inbox, the Board's drawer and the sealed chat's row can open one; a cleared store loses the tab, never the task). A separate copy's task takes the chat's place in the strip; a pass of one opens to its right. `StripTab` has a `task` kind whose state comes from `TaskSessionDto.board` (`task-tab-state.ts`); closing one pushes a `ClosedTask` onto the closed stack, so ⌘⇧T, All Tabs and Open Session bring it back. `TaskTab` (`task-tab.tsx`) reads `GET /tasks/:id/session` (`daemon/task-session-queries.ts`) and draws a separate copy (`copy-tab.tsx`: the chat it came from, the brief, the task's thread, then one line from `task-tab-words.ts`) or a pass of one (`pass-tab.tsx`). A sealed chat (`conversation.state === 'sealed'`) has no composer and no Do It…: its line says where the work went, with Open Task; its transcript's `handed_to` notice is a divider with Open.
- A pass refused only because chats' turns hold Your folder offers **Stop the Turn and Open** and **Open When It Ends** (`when-busy.ts`: every holder is a chat's turn; a lane, a takeover, 10db's git work or another pass offers neither), in the Do It sheet (the daemon's `here.whenBusy`; the sheet's Do It becomes those two buttons) and on the Workbench's Start Pass (`whenBusy` on the open request, read from `useProjectFolder`). An open that waits answers `pending`: there is no task yet, and `FolderDto.pendingOpen` draws `pending-open-line.tsx` ("Opens when … ends its turn · Cancel") on the Workbench and the session bar until the folder route drops it.
- A pass of one's tab (`pass-tab.tsx`) is the Workbench's page: `PagePane` (thread, composer, Sign Off, Retry, the pull request button) from the live pass (`usePassView`), with `ClosedPassLine`, `CloseProgress`, `ClosePassModal` and `AbandonModal` reused. Its bar says "Task · Your folder · lands on `main`", and Close Pass… shows once a page is signed off (filled when nothing works); Abandon Pass… is in ⋯. The Workbench shows a live pass for a project with no page area. Every area name the builder reads goes through `areaLabel`, so `10db-whole-project` reads "Whole project".
- Changes (`changes-panel.tsx`, `changes-files.ts`; ⌘⇧D is `changes` in `session-keys.ts`, heard by `TaskTab`): a task's `GET /workstreams/:id/changes?scope=all|last`, refetched when its workstream's `updatedAt` moves. The bar's "Changes +41 −6" opens a panel beside the thread; it opens by itself once at 1100 px when there are changes, and the builder's choice then holds for the tab. A pass of one adds All Turns · Last Turn. `parseChanges` cuts the unified diff into files and hunks, each drawn by the kit's `HunkDiff`, whose line numbers are buttons that put `@path:line` and the line, quoted, into the task's composer (`withQuote`). Not built: the split view from 720 px, the check line under the header, and the base's name.
- Open in Terminal (sessions UI spec §10): `terminal-button.tsx` (the session bar's `terminal-2` icon, shown with `capabilities.takeover` and not on a sealed chat or one already in the terminal) opens `terminal-popover.tsx`, which reads `GET …/takeover/preview` for the daemon's notes and refusal and confirms with Enter. `ChatTab` shows `terminal-banner.tsx` while the conversation is `taken_over` (Hand Back behind its question, Copy Command through `POST …/takeover/command`, focus on Hand Back), the kind line says "in your terminal since {HH:mm}", the composer reads "Held until you hand back…" and each unsent message shows "Held until you hand back". Archiving such a chat asks "End takeover and archive?" in `sessions-view.tsx`. Settings › Sessions saves the terminal choice through `PATCH /settings/sessions` (`daemon/sessions-settings-queries.ts`). The copy says zsh or bash for the pasted line, never fish. A `taken_over` chat's tab is its own state (`in_terminal`: a dimmed `terminal-2`, a dimmed title, "in your terminal" spoken and in the Recent line). A clipboard that refuses the command leaves it in the banner to copy by hand (the popover is gone by then).
