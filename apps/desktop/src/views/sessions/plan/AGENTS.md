# apps/desktop/src/views/sessions/plan

A session's Plan: the panel beside its conversation, and the Record view. Plan has no screen of its own: a Plan belongs to a session (`ChatDto.planId`), and every link to a plan opens that session (`useOpenSession`, `sessions/open-session.tsx`, which places the tab and brings an archived one back; a failure shows through `useOpenSessionNotice`). Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-10db-plan-in-sessions-design.md`.

- There is one New Session. A fresh tab's title row has the Plan toggle (`setFreshPlan` in `fresh-tabs.ts`), so the builder opens the Plan before the first message; ⌘⇧N on any view and Factory's New Session buttons (`useNewPlan` in `sessions/start-fresh-tab.ts`) open a fresh tab with the Plan already on (`plan: true`). Its first send, or first dropped file, creates the session with `useCreateChat({ plan: true })`; dropped files then upload to `POST /shapes/:id/files` in order, a second send or drop joins the create in flight, a tab closed mid-create lets it finish and then closes the session (Recently closed holds it), and a failed create leaves the tab fresh with its draft and files and a Try Again. Reasons files were refused go to the session through `drop-notes.ts`.
- `plan-side.tsx` places the panel: beside the conversation in a Mantine `Splitter` when the tab holds 380 px of conversation and 340 px of panel, else (a very narrow tab only) a `SurfaceDrawer` about 560 px wide closed by "Close Plan"; with Changes open too its head switches between the two. The divider is the splitter's hairline: the builder drags the panel between 340 and 960 px (440 until dragged, and again on a double-click), one width for every session, remembered (`tendb.plan.width`, through `shell/stored-width.ts`), drawn narrower on a tab that would leave the conversation under 380 px. The conversation is always the splitter's first pane, so opening the panel never remounts it. The split runs out to the frame's edges, so the divider is full height; the conversation's pane gives the frame's padding back, and every edge of text sits 40 px from the frame or the divider: the frame's 24 px and the list's 16 on the conversation's side, the panel's own 40 px inset on its side (its list 28 px plus a row's 12, which holds its hover tone). `plan-choice.ts` keeps each session's open state (a session with a plan opens it by default), the title row's Plan toggle flips it (on every session, `useShowPlan` making the plan on its first press, unless the conversation is sealed or its project removed; a session with a plan always has it; the tooltip reads Show Plan or Close Plan), and an open panel folds the Sessions panel to the rail (`sessions-view.tsx` stops drawing it). `plan-panel.tsx` wraps `useShapeView(chat.planId)` and `proposed-pane.tsx`: header (Plan 18 px, then Accept, an expand button that opens the Plan page, and ⋯, ruled off from the list edge to edge; ⋯ holds a Review section (Plan Review, Find Open Questions, Design Review, each with what it buys; the ⋯ spins while one is asked for, and a dim line under the head says what it did) and then Export Plan to Folder (a dialog saying what it writes, then the folder panel) and Undo Accept, always there, whose dim line says why when it is off (nothing accepted yet, or a task of it has started); the title row's Plan toggle and the session menu close it), the pinned `summary-line.tsx` (one dim line from the second revision on: "Revision 2 · 1 goal · 2 tasks · 1 to record · revised just now · 4 changed this round"; a first proposal has none, since the list says it all; the last part is left out when every row changed; each count is a jump), `proposed-list.tsx`, `preview-panel.tsx` and `heard-so-far.tsx` while there are no rows. The panel keeps the conversation's surface, and so do its sections, apart by space alone: each goal under its bright title with its tasks as `goal-task-row.tsx` rows, then a folding `PlanSection` (`plan-section.tsx`: a dim name and count, the chevron hanging in the gutter) for Tasks (Other tasks beside goals: tasks under no goal; a plan of only Tasks draws no head), Changes, Landings, Decisions, Questions for others, How it would run and Earlier. The pane keeps which are folded (How it would run and Earlier start folded); a summary count's jump unfolds them all. Only an opened row is raised, on the panel tone (`--tendb-color-panel`, bg_1 in both schemes, the composer's). A row (`row-line.tsx`) is one line in grays: the title as a button that covers the row, dim words at the right (the round's mark as its 8 px dot then its word, a kind such as Added or Principle, Spec first, a pin, "2 notes" in words), the time column ("~0.5 h", "About 0.5 h of agent time" on hover), then a chevron shown on hover or focus and turned while open. Opening a row changes nothing on its line but the chevron and the title's colour, so nothing moves; the block opens under it through Mantine's `Collapse` (150 ms, unmounted when closed), and the row's pencil and ⋯ sit at the right of the block's facts line. Its ⋯ has Edit, Keep My Version / Let the Agent Change It (pin), Show in Conversation and Remove from Plan; the editor offers Estimated Size and How It's Built in words. The round's mark shows from the second round on: in the first every row is new. Only one row of the panel is open at a time (`OpenRow` in `proposed-list.tsx`); a click or Enter opens it in place and ↑/↓ move between the rows of the open sections. An opened row is one block (`row-details.tsx`), its title bright: in 16 px steps: description, Acceptance Criteria (bulleted), one dim line of facts ("Area: board · about 0.5 h of agent time") ending in the Show in Conversation link (the conversation scrolls to the messages the row came from and marks them in the accent), its mock-up (a 160 px thumbnail: the page drawn 960 px wide and scaled down to the row, fading at its foot, with an expand mark; a click opens it full size in a modal) labelled Mock-up with a green Approved once approved, then Approve (light) and Ask for Changes, or Ask for Changes and a quiet Remove Approval; then last the review's notes (`ReviewNotes`: "Review Notes · n", each on a left rule, yellow for medium or high, "Design Review · Medium" with a visible Dismiss, its text at full strength, three lines until More, three notes until Show More). Backtick spans in the description and notes read as code (`record/code-spans.tsx`). A goal's task is matched to its mock-ups by key, never by position. The run preview is the "How it would run" section, only for a plan of two tasks or more; it draws only the lanes that have a task (the simulation returns one lane per lane the project allows), its totals at the head only while folded, and a critical path only across two lanes or more. `row-editor.tsx` edits in place; an edit is measured against the row as the editor opened, and when a round changes the row under it the editor says "The agent changed this" and Save pins only the builder's fields. Accept in `proposed-pane.tsx` is one button; its modal (Accept Plan) counts what would start from `useStartCounts` and ends in Accept (on the Board, nothing starts) and the primary Accept and Start, which is left out, with Accept the primary, when nothing would start; the counts line shows only when something would wait or stay. A row there says where it goes; open notes are one warning with Read Them, which closes the modal and opens the first ticked row with notes in the panel (the pane owns the open row). A refused start keeps the modal open on Done. The panel's ⋯ menu has Undo Accept (`UndoAcceptModal` in `undo-modals.tsx`), disabled with its reason before any Accept or when a task of `view.lastAccept` has a run in the Board's task cache; the daemon refuses on its own. Accept (`convert-modal.tsx`) lists the pending rows, the cut list for a Plan of more than five goals, warnings and a 400's errors; it works while a turn runs and never stops it. The transcript maps a plan session's `shape_event` items with `plan-entries.ts` (`planEventEntry`, given to the kit's `itemEntries` as `own`); a session's files are dropped into its composer, which sends them to the plan.
- A proposal row's state dot (`rowDot` in `proposal-text.ts`) is the first of Accepted, the round's mark (New or Changed this round, Moved to Earlier), Pinned, else Proposed.
- `plan-screen.tsx` is what a session's `see_screen` draws for the Plan (`/plan-screen/$shapeId`, inert outside the render frame): the conversation beside the panel, read only. `open-plan-session.tsx` is Open Session on a task's or goal's drawer. Plan's measures (`views/settings/plan-measures.tsx`) are in Settings › Planning.
- An opened row shows its attachments (`attachments/`): a mockup in an `<iframe sandbox="">` through `srcdoc` under `MOCKUP_CSP`; a chart (checked again with `ChartSpec`, drawn by Vega-Embed with `refusingLoader` and `vega-interpreter`, since the window's CSP has no `unsafe-eval`) and a Mermaid diagram (`securityLevel: 'strict'`, SVG labels; only the kinds in `DRAWN_KINDS`, and refused if the parsed diagram names an image or its text a CSS load) drawn to SVG and shown as an `<img>`, which runs no script and loads nothing.
- Remove Draft Goal is also in the Board's goal line menu ("More goal actions") and Factory's open goal (`remove-draft-goal.tsx`, `useRemoveDraftGoalAction`: a goal a plan made whose status is Not started; the same `RemoveDraftGoalModal`).
- A Plan's record (`views/sessions/plan/record/`, `daemon/plan-record-queries.ts`, decision `decisions/2026-11-12-a-plan-keeps-its-whole-record-locally.md`) is the Plan page, its own route, `/sessions/record/$shapeId` (`tab`, `doc` (the document's id, since a goal's spec and its eval share a key), `rev` and `section` in the address, each kept only when it parses, `record-tabs.ts`), reached from the panel head's expand button (Open Plan Page). Its title row is a back button to the session, Plan, and the session's title dim, with Export to Folder. Its tabs (`RECORD_TABS`, each with its count; an empty, unfiltered list is one dim line, not an empty table) are Plan (the default: the panel's `PlanPanel` with `full`, untitled, the summary beside Accept and ⋯, its rows' text 16 px in, under the tabs' labels and the tables' first column; Where it came from goes back to the session, and Ask for Changes puts its words in the session's draft and goes back), Documents (a document list, a revision `Select`, the outline indented by level, and the body drawn whole through one `Markdown`, so a footnote or a reference link keeps its definition, and a picked section scrolls to its heading, which `Markdown` marks with its source line as `data-line`), Decisions (a row opens what was turned down and the builder's words), Sources (each anchor with each task's newest check, named by the task and followed by where and when it read as text, "at <commit> · <date>": `anchor-status.ts`, Current green only when found at its line, Moved yellow, Changed red, Not checked gray, a page "Read <date>"; a superseded fact's badges are dimmed; State and Kind filters), Review Notes (grouped by what was reviewed, named by its title on the Plan, then revision and a later review's number, each group's header counted over the whole filtered list, in the reviewer's words; an open note has Dismiss and Ask to Fix, which puts the note in the session's reply and goes back), Questions and On the Board (each task Accept made, its section, which opens Documents there, and its state as Factory says it, or Cancelled, or Removed when the project no longer has it). A reviewer's, a question's and a decision's words draw their backtick code spans as code (`code-spans.tsx`). A list that fails says why in its table's body, so its header filters stay in reach. Every list is a daemon page in the Knowledge tables' style (`KnowledgeTable`), filters in the headers. Export to Folder (`export-button.tsx`) writes the record as Markdown; it takes a folder from the native panel, or a typed path in a browser, and shows the daemon's refusal, or the panel's own failure, under the button. Accept shows and starts no rehearsal: Find Open Questions returns with the Review menu. Words that name the agent (the composer's "Couldn't reach <agent>", the header's research-and-review line) use the conversation's `capabilities.label`; the sidebar's hints name none. A goal Accept made says "Planned in <session>" (`factory/planned-in.tsx`), a link that opens that session, and a task's drawer has a Plan context disclosure (`board/plan-context-disclosure.tsx`) showing, byte for byte, the block its lane got (`GET /tasks/:taskId/plan-context`, stored when packed, so a re-import never changes it) and its anchor checks with Evidence's badges, a check from another run than the one that got the block saying "checked by an earlier run". Markdown is drawn only by `conversation/`'s `Markdown` (streamdown, GFM only: raw HTML shown as its text and never rendered, so `<host>` or `Promise<void>` reads as written, sanitized, images replaced by "Image not shown", an `https:` link a link with no `href` (its address in its title and a closure, so no middle-click, context menu or drag opens it) whose click or Enter asks the builder to confirm its host and then opens it in the browser (`openWebLink`), and any other link its text; a fence is `CodeHighlight` on Shiki's JavaScript regex engine with Selenized themes). A text longer than `MARKDOWN_MAX_LENGTH` (512 Ki) is shown whole as a plain code block instead, and `MarkdownBoundary` shows the same when a drawing throws, as the recursive parser does on a text nested thousands deep.

- An accepted goal or task row shows its tasks' live state: `useLiveWords` reads the project's tasks and the workstreams the Board reads, `live-state.ts` turns them into Factory's words (Running, Queued, Needs you, In review, Ready, Waiting, Done, Removed), and `rowDot` shows Running or Done from them (Accepted otherwise). "Open on Board" opens the task, a goal's first task not done, or Factory's goal.
- ⋯'s Review section (`proposed-pane.tsx`) is Plan review and Find Open Questions over every pending goal and task (the panel has no per-row ticks: ticks are the Accept modal's); a line under the head says what it did. Findings and questions land on rows (`review-notes.tsx`) and in the conversation as `report_finding` lines. A mock-up under a row has Approve (light) or Undo Approval (`attachments/row-attachments.tsx`, `useApproveAttachment`); a mock-up's versions (rows on one key) show as one: the newest, labelled "Mock-up · Version n", carries the buttons, and the older ones fold behind Earlier Versions (n), which names an approved one while folded; an approved mock-up shows in its task's drawer Plan context (`board/plan-context-disclosure.tsx`), also for a task with no document pin.
- Ask for Changes sits beside Approve on a mock-up (`attachments/row-attachments.tsx`): it adds `Change the mock-up for "<task title>": ` to the session's composer draft through `AttachmentsContext.onAskForChanges` (the chat tab's draft) and focuses the composer, closing a drawer panel first. It sends nothing. The file picker has no `accept`: the input refuses only size and count, and the daemon's words show for any other refusal.
- The Review section also holds Design review (`POST /shapes/:id/design-review`): the head's line says it is taking pictures until the daemon has them and queues the turn; "Nothing to review: no task has both a mock-up and a page." when none qualifies. The task drawer's Plan context shows the newest design check (`views/board/design-check-view.tsx`: mock-up beside the app per route, then the agent's verdict), refetched on `task.changed`.
