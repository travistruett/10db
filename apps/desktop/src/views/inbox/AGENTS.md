# apps/desktop/src/views/inbox

- The Inbox (`views/inbox/`) shows every project's cards, each naming its project (`shell/project-label.tsx`), narrowed by its own Project filter (`?project=`, `shell/project-filter.tsx`, shared with Factory); the Open count follows the filter, while the sidebar badge counts every project, and 10db's own cards show under any filter. It shows one row per card in the panel's list, except that identical dismissable flags (same project, title and body, no options) share a row with a count, and a Workbench pass's Ready pages (`check` cards whose detail is a `PassCheckDetail`) share one row per pass; the daemon groups them (`inboxRowKey` in contracts) and the desktop only places the rows it is sent. The selected row's card is the content beside the panel: a pass's content is "Workbench: n pages ready" with Open Workbench (Enter on the selected row too), which selects the pass's project and opens the Workbench, and each page's title, which opens that page there, with its Sign Off (`pass-ready-row.tsx`, the same sign-off route as the page's composer), which offers neither Approve nor Send Back; a failed turn's flag keeps its own row. A row (`inbox-row-item.tsx`) is two lines: the kind tag (`KIND_TAGS`), the title, `×n` and the age, then the project and the body's first line (a chat's card shows its session's title instead, a pass's row its page count); a flag's row offers Dismiss on hover and focus. Every other card a pass raises is drawn by its title, body and options; `pass-card-extras.tsx` (an exhaustive switch over `PassCardDetail`'s kinds) adds what the builder acts on outside 10db: a branch the close could not move shows its merge command with Copy command, and a follow-on card links the pull request the later sign-offs went to. Its keys are in `use-inbox-keys.ts` and the list the ? button opens is in `inbox-shortcuts.tsx`; change both together.
- Nothing from the system check goes above a view; it is sorted by what the builder can do (`views/inbox/system-cards.ts`, `decisions/2026-11-08-the-inbox-holds-what-needs-the-builder.md`). What needs the builder (a missing required tool, a refused pinned copy with Fetch It Again, an unreadable login shell, a refused Artificial Analysis key) is a 10db card at the top of the Inbox's Open view (`system-card-list.tsx`): it counts in Open n, the sidebar badge and the menu bar (`inbox-count.ts`), is never answered or resolved, and leaves once a check no longer finds it. A copy being downloaded is a dimmed line in the header (`shell/agent-download-status.tsx`). The release each copy runs, a newer release and a version warning are told in Settings › Updates (`views/settings/agent-copies-section.tsx`), which says there is nothing to do. `useSystemCheck` never asks in a screen's frame, whose token may not read `/system`.
- `InboxView` draws the title row once for every state (`TitleRow`: "Inbox", "n open", the Project filter and the always-rendered xs subtle `?` button) and the panel (`inbox-panel.tsx`: the head, then an `Open / Waiting / Resolved` `SegmentedControl`, `aria-label="Inbox state"`, with counts, then the list). The state is the URL's `?state=` (`inboxSearch` in `inbox-search.ts`; Open leaves no key). The panel draws a host element for the list and `OpenInbox` (`open-inbox.tsx`) portals its `InboxRowList` into it, so the switch stays mounted as the state changes. Open's keys, drafts and answers live in `OpenInbox`; `open-content.tsx` draws the selected row (`InboxCard` with `framed={false}`, a pass's Ready pages, or a 10db card) and `card-context.tsx` a question's last messages. Selection follows a row's key (`nextSelection`): answering or dismissing selects the row now at that index, and `j`/`k` past the loaded end read the next page and move once when it lands. An answer that gets a 409 shows "This card was already answered." over the content until the selection moves again, and looks the card up in every list so its row leaves. A row pick closes the panel when it is a sheet (`closeSheet` in `FrameSlots`). Never draw a header inside a view, or switching moves it. The ? key works only on Open.
- Waiting and Resolved are tables the daemon pages (`GET /inbox/page` through `useInboxPage`; the route is in `apps/daemon/src/inbox/AGENTS.md`): the daemon filters, sorts and counts, and the client never loads every card to do it. Both draw `InboxTable` (`inbox-table.tsx`, on mantine-datatable), whose filtering and sorting headers come from `shell/column-header.tsx` and whose pager is `pagerProps` with `usePaging` keyed on the filters and sort, so a change goes back to page 1. The Inbox's Project filter (`?project=`) goes in the query. A row opens on a click, or on the focused row with Enter or → (← closes it, ↓ and ↑ move between rows; `row-keys.ts`); a cell's own buttons and menus keep their clicks and keys (`ActionsCell`). The tables sit inside `data-inbox-keys-ignore`, so Open's keys never act on them.
  - Resolved (`resolved-inbox.tsx`): Kind (filter Any Kind, sortable), Project, Title (sortable), Outcome (filter Any Outcome: Answered, Closed by 10db, Slack Answer, from `inboxOutcome` in contracts, the same rule the daemon's filter uses) and When (sortable, newest first by default). An open row (`resolved-detail.tsx`) shows how it closed: a Slack answer's lines (who, how long after asking, how it was read, their words) with its thread link, 10db's reason or the builder's answer, a failed send's words (`send-failure.ts`), and the principle links described below.
  - Waiting (`waiting-inbox.tsx`, `waiting-row.tsx`): With (filter Anyone and each person), Question (sortable), Project, How Long (sent and reminded, sortable, the longest out first by default) and the row's thread link and menu (Answer Myself opens the row; Take Back asks first). An open row shows the message as sent, the conversation and the Answer Myself buttons. While the Slack connection loads it draws nothing, and while Slack is not connected it says "Slack isn't connected, so questions sent there wait in Open." and asks for no page: those cards are the builder's, in Open (`isWaiting`). The Waiting count is the daemon's total for a page of one (`useInboxCount`), shown only while Slack is connected.
  - The Sessions chat tab reads its project's latest page of resolved cards (100) so a card answered in its transcript shows its answer.
- A `check` card in the Inbox (`views/inbox/`) is the builder's check of finished work in a project with no gate command (`decisions/2026-10-28-optional-gate.md`): it loads the task's changes from `GET /workstreams/:id/changes` and answers Approve, or Send Back with a required note. The board shows the waiting task as Blocked.
- A `plan` card (`plan-card.tsx`) is a plan waiting for the builder before Build starts, raised for any task whose rigor row has `approvesPlan`: it shows the plan and answers Approve, or Send Back with a required note, which reruns the Plan step.
- The Inbox keys (`use-inbox-keys.ts`) ignore anything inside `data-inbox-keys-ignore`, and a check or plan card is answered with its own buttons, never the digits or Enter.
- Knowledge on Inbox cards (`views/inbox/`): a principle proposal card (`isPrincipleCard`: a proposal whose detail has a `principleId`) shows its verdicts in words (`PRINCIPLE_VERDICT_LABELS`: This project, All my projects, This area (name), Reject) and the principle's kind as a badge; the kind and area come from `GET /projects/:id/proposals` (`principle-card.ts`), so the area choice reads plain "This area" until that loads. The daemon still gets the stable value (`accept_area`). A question card shows `RecordDecision` above the note: a checkbox, checked for product, design and engineering (`defaultRecording`, the daemon's own default) and a scope select (This project, All my projects, Area: name). A question's answer always carries `record` (`null` when unchecked, so nothing is recorded); every other kind and the Park the task choice send none, and the control goes while Park the task is chosen. The record choice lives in the card's draft like the note, and digit keys and Enter keep working until a field has focus. An answered question whose note said always or never has `detail.proposedPrinciples` (`CARD_PROPOSED_PRINCIPLES`, written by the daemon in the answer's transaction); the Resolved view then says a principle was proposed and links to `/knowledge?principle=<id>` after selecting the card's project (one link per principle; several read Open principle 1, 2…). `ProjectKnowledge` opens that principle in `ProvenanceDrawer`, which works in any state and names it by the provenance's `text`, on the Principles tab under Pending while its state is proposed, Past once it no longer applies, and Current otherwise; closing the drawer drops the id from the URL. A resolved card says its answer through `optionLabel` (the area verdict reads plain "This area", the proposal no longer being pending), and a choice no option matches reads as stored.
