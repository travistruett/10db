# apps/desktop/src/views/inbox

- The Inbox (`views/inbox/`) shows every project's cards, each naming its project (`shell/project-label.tsx`), narrowed by its own Project filter (`?project=`, in the panel's Filter menu); the Open count follows the filter, while the sidebar badge counts every project, and 10db's own cards show under the project filter, and hide while a search or a kind is set. It shows one row per card in the panel's list, except that identical dismissable flags (same project, title and body, no options) share a row with a count, and a Workbench pass's Ready pages (`check` cards whose detail is a `PassCheckDetail`) share one row per pass; the daemon groups them (`inboxRowKey` in contracts) and the desktop only places the rows it is sent. The selected row's card is the content beside the panel: a pass's content is "Workbench: n pages ready" with Open Workbench (Enter on the selected row too), which selects the pass's project and opens the Workbench, and each page's title, which opens that page there, with its Sign Off (`pass-ready-row.tsx`, the same sign-off route as the page's composer), which offers neither Approve nor Send Back; a failed turn's flag keeps its own row. A row (`inbox-row-item.tsx`) is two lines, 64 px: the kind's icon (`KIND_ICONS` in `kind-icons.ts`, named by `KIND_LABELS`), the title, `×n` and the age, then the project and the body's first line (a chat's card shows its session's title instead, a pass's row its page count); a flag's row offers Dismiss on hover (focus follows the selection, whose card has its own), drawn beside the row's option by `renderAction` (an option holds no nested control). Every other card a pass raises is drawn by its title, body and options. Its keys are in `use-inbox-keys.ts` and the list the ? button opens is in `inbox-shortcuts.tsx`; change both together.
- The live list (`daemon/inbox-live.ts`, `inbox-queries.ts`): rows are read by keyset (`after`), 50 a page, under `['inbox-rows', list]`. An `inbox` event patches the loaded pages one row at a time: `refreshCard` asks `GET /inbox/page?card=<id>` and `mergeRow` puts the row the daemon answers where its sort belongs (past the loaded end it is dropped, the next page brings it), and the card leaves every row it was in; applying a change twice gives the same pages. A list nobody is looking at is marked stale and reloads when shown. A lookup waits for a fetch in flight so an event racing a page is not lost. A whole refetch happens only on reconnect, on a Slack connection change, or after a failed lookup. The desktop never groups: `joinRows` only puts each row's card ids onto the cards the page holds (`decisions/2026-11-17-the-daemon-groups-inbox-rows-in-its-code-and-the-browser-groups-nothing.md`). A grouped row matches a search when any of its cards does.
- Counts: `useInboxCount(state)` is the `total` of one row of `GET /inbox/page` with no filter (so a filter or search never changes it), and `useOpenInboxCount` (`inbox-count.ts`) adds 10db's own cards for the sidebar badge, the title row and the menu bar. Keys (`use-inbox-keys.ts`): `j`/`k` and the arrows move between rows inside `data-inbox-list` (`LIST_ATTRIBUTE`), digits choose an option, `n` the note, `e` dismisses, Enter submits, `?` opens the shortcuts, and ⌘F focuses the panel's search; anything inside `data-inbox-keys-ignore` is left alone.
- Still read from `GET /inbox` (every open card as an array): the answered-notice and notifications in `desktop/sync.ts`, Shape's waiting permissions, and the daemon's own tests (`useInbox`). The Inbox's lists never read it.
- Nothing from the system check goes above a view; it is sorted by what the builder can do (`views/inbox/system-cards.ts`, `decisions/2026-11-08-the-inbox-holds-what-needs-the-builder.md`). What needs the builder (a missing required tool, a refused pinned copy with Fetch It Again, an unreadable login shell, a refused Artificial Analysis key) is a 10db card at the top of the Inbox's Open view (`system-card-list.tsx`): it counts in Open n, the sidebar badge and the menu bar (`inbox-count.ts`), is never answered or resolved, and leaves once a check no longer finds it. A copy being downloaded is a dimmed line in the header (`shell/agent-download-status.tsx`). The release each copy runs, a newer release and a version warning are told in Settings › Updates (`views/settings/agent-copies-section.tsx`), which says there is nothing to do. `useSystemCheck` never asks in a screen's frame, whose token may not read `/system`.
- `InboxView` draws the title row once for every state (`TitleRow`: the list it shows, Open, Waiting or Resolved, since the panel's head says Inbox, and the always-rendered xs subtle `?` button) and the panel (`inbox-panel.tsx`: the head, with the filter, then an `Open / Waiting / Resolved` `SegmentedControl`, `aria-label="Inbox state"`, with counts, then the list). The state is the URL's `?state=` (`inboxSearch` in `inbox-search.ts`; Open leaves no key). The panel draws a host element for the list and `OpenInbox` (`open-inbox.tsx`) portals its `InboxRowList` into it, so the switch stays mounted as the state changes. Open's keys, drafts and answers live in `OpenInbox`; `open-content.tsx` draws the selected row (`InboxCard` with `framed={false}`, a pass's Ready pages, or a 10db card); an unframed card fills the content's height, opens with `CardHeader` and ends in `CardActions`, a bar held at the content's foot and `card-context.tsx` a question's last messages. Selection follows a row's key (`nextSelection`): answering or dismissing selects the row now at that index, and `j`/`k` past the loaded end read the next page and move once when it lands. An answer that gets a 409 shows "This card was already answered." over the content until the selection moves again, and looks the card up in every list so its row leaves. A row pick closes the panel when it is a sheet (`closeSheet` in `FrameSlots`). Coming back: `InboxView` writes its search to `lastInboxSearch` (the rail's Inbox link carries it), and Open stores its selected key and list scroll in `inboxStateMemory` (`rememberSelected`, `rememberScroll`); the list takes focus on the selected row when the builder arrives with focus on the page or the rail, and moves it with the selection while it is on a row, never otherwise. The next row's content is prefetched (`prefetch-next-row.ts`: a question's context, a check's changes). A screen frame (`RenderLayout`) draws the panel column only while a view registers a panel. Never draw a header inside a view, or switching moves it. The ? key works only on Open.
- Waiting and Resolved are lists the daemon pages by cursor, as Open is (`useInboxRows`; the route is in `apps/daemon/src/inbox/AGENTS.md`): `ListedInbox` (`listed-inbox.tsx`) draws the state's `InboxRowList` into the panel and the selected row's content beside it, with `nextSelection`, the remembered row and scroll, and j/k. The panel's head holds the search (`inbox-search-field.tsx`: it keeps its own text, writes `?q=` 250 ms after the last keystroke with `replace`, follows a `q` changed from outside, and ⌘F focuses it; the listener is the field's, so it lives while the Inbox is shown) and the Filter menu (`inbox-filter-menu.tsx`: Project, Kind, With on Waiting, Outcome and When on Resolved, each a check on the right, Clear Filters). Filters live in the URL (`?project=&kind=&person=&outcome=&since=`); `inboxList` leaves out the ones a state does not use, and `filtersSet` and `narrowed` read the same rule. A change of `q` or a filter changes the list key; `useInboxRows` keeps the old rows until the new first page arrives, and the selection keeps its key when the new rows hold it, else takes the first row. 10db's own cards are hidden while `q` or `kind` is set. An empty list says its state's words ("Nothing needs you", "Nothing is waiting on anyone", "Nothing resolved yet"), or "No matches" with Clear Filters when narrowed (`inbox-empty.tsx`).
  - Resolved: a row's second line is the project and "<outcome> · <answer>" (`OUTCOME_LABELS` in `inbox-row-item.tsx`, from `inboxOutcome` in contracts, the rule the daemon's filter uses; the answer is the option's words, or "Dismissed"), its age is when it closed. Its content (`resolved-content.tsx`, `resolved-detail.tsx`) shows how it closed: a Slack answer's lines (who, how long after asking, how it was read, their words) with its thread link, 10db's reason or the builder's answer, a failed send's words (`send-failure.ts`), and the principle links described below.
  - Waiting (`waiting-content.tsx`, `waiting-row.tsx`): a row's second line is the project and "Waiting on <recipients> · <how long ago>" (`recipientsOf`), its age is since sent, the longest out first. Its content shows who it waits on and since when, the message as sent, the conversation, the Answer Myself buttons, the thread link and Take It Back (which asks first). While the Slack connection loads it draws nothing, and while Slack is not connected it says "Slack isn't connected, so questions sent there wait in Open." and asks for no page: those cards are the builder's, in Open (`isWaiting`).
  - The Sessions chat tab reads its project's latest page of resolved cards (100) so a card answered in its transcript shows its answer.
- A `check` card in the Inbox (`views/inbox/`) is the builder's check of finished work in a project with no gate command (`decisions/2026-10-28-optional-gate.md`): it loads the task's changes from `GET /workstreams/:id/changes` and answers Approve, or Send Back with a required note. The board shows the waiting task as Blocked.
- A `plan` card (`plan-card.tsx`) is a plan waiting for the builder before Build starts, raised for any task whose rigor row has `approvesPlan`: it shows the plan and answers Approve, or Send Back with a required note, which reruns the Plan step.
- The Inbox keys (`use-inbox-keys.ts`) ignore anything inside `data-inbox-keys-ignore`, and a check or plan card is answered with its own buttons, never the digits or Enter.
- Knowledge on Inbox cards (`views/inbox/`): a principle proposal card (`isPrincipleCard`: a proposal whose detail has a `principleId`) shows its verdicts in words (`PRINCIPLE_VERDICT_LABELS`: This project, All my projects, This area (name), Reject) and the principle's kind as a badge; the kind and area come from `GET /projects/:id/proposals` (`principle-card.ts`), so the area choice reads plain "This area" until that loads. The daemon still gets the stable value (`accept_area`). A question card shows `RecordDecision` above the note: a checkbox, checked for product, design and engineering (`defaultRecording`, the daemon's own default) and a scope select (This project, All my projects, Area: name). A question's answer always carries `record` (`null` when unchecked, so nothing is recorded); every other kind and the Park the task choice send none, and the control goes while Park the task is chosen. The record choice lives in the card's draft like the note, and digit keys and Enter keep working until a field has focus. An answered question whose note said always or never has `detail.proposedPrinciples` (`CARD_PROPOSED_PRINCIPLES`, written by the daemon in the answer's transaction); the Resolved view then says a principle was proposed and links to `/knowledge?principle=<id>` after selecting the card's project (one link per principle; several read Open principle 1, 2…). `ProjectKnowledge` opens that principle in `ProvenanceDrawer`, which works in any state and names it by the provenance's `text`, on the Principles tab under Pending while its state is proposed, Past once it no longer applies, and Current otherwise; closing the drawer drops the id from the URL. A resolved card says its answer through `optionLabel` (the area verdict reads plain "This area", the proposal no longer being pending), and a choice no option matches reads as stored.
- A condition card (`detail.closesItself`, `CARD_CLOSES_ITSELF`: its `closesWhen` is `by: 'event'`; one 10db withdraws, or the builder answers, keeps its Dismiss) is never dismissed:
  `isDismissable` is false for it, so the row offers no Dismiss or Dismiss All, `e` does nothing, and `InboxCard` draws no
  button when it has no options (the daemon refuses a dismiss with a 409). Its options still send an answer. A card whose
  `detail.opens` is a `CardOpens` shows its link (`card-opens-link.tsx`): a Settings section through the router, or a
  `file://` link for a path, as the step log's is.
